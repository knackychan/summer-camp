# Kitchen Quest v0.7 — cooking progression

Requested by Papa after the v0.6 integration: add complexity and depth along the existing cooking-game direction. This release extends the native pixel kitchen and its existing stations.

## Scope

- Four recipes unlock after 4, 8, 12 and 16 completed orders: Pickle Crunch Burger, Double Stack Burger, Chef Salad, and Lasagna Feast. Existing recipes stay available. New recipes introduce two patties on one dish, longer sequences, and combining oven food with vegetables.
- Kitchen Shift introduces deterministic customer requests after the introductory orders: extra tomato, extra pickles, or no cheese. The ticket, read-aloud instructions and grading all use the requested sequence. Very Easy keeps ordinary recipes and its generous cooking windows.
- Repeatable shift goals reward variety and preparation: four dishes/two recipes; five dishes including salad and lasagna; six dishes including a double-stack burger. Completing a goal starts the next goal without discarding dishes or kitchen stock.
- A cookbook shows exact recipes, unlock requirements, and each child's served counts. Profile progress lives in the existing local settings save (`settings.kitchen.profiles[kid]`), separate from session-only dishes. Previous served bests provide a conservative starting total on first migration. Existing `best.kitchen` remains the best session order count.
- A Prep tab compares both open dishes with ready food, reserved patties, batches underway and shortages. Burnt reserved patties are called out for retry. Planning does not change inventory or recipe grading.
- No spendable currency, customer abandonment, new network dependency or change to the family reward ledger.

## Acceptance

- Successful serves update progress exactly once; mistakes, duplicate actions and abandoned dishes do not award progress.
- Unlocks reach the live recipe deck without rewriting current tickets. Switching difficulty preserves cookbook progress; canceling confirmation preserves dishes.
- Saved data is validated and isolated by child. Cookbook and confirmation pause the entire cooking clock; closing a book that was backgrounded must not silently resume the game.
- Every new child-facing string is English and Traditional Chinese. Existing pixel palette, touch targets, responsive layouts, shared navigation and offline support remain.
- Domain, preparation, real-browser gameplay, offline reload, and Android web bundle checks pass.

Status: implemented. Physical tablet acceptance remains a separate device check.
