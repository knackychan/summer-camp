## What it is
Brick Lab grows from 26 parts in 8 categories to 217 parts in 18 — a catalog big enough to build a house, a tree, a car, a castle, a pirate ship or a moon base: more bricks, plates, tiles and slopes; round bricks, cones and domes; arches, columns, stairs, fences, doors and windows; vehicle parts; levers, hinges, turntables and gears; plants; minifigures; animals; hats and helmets; and theme props for home & town, castle, pirates and space. The look is Lego's (studs, real proportions on the 8 mm pitch), the parts are our own code-drawn shapes.

## Decisions
| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Parts are data.** A new part is a list of primitives — box, cylinder, cone, ball, dome, torus, lathe, prism (a 2-D outline pushed along x, y or z), studs — each with a position, turn, scale and a colour slot. One builder (`makeModelPiece`) turns that list into **one merged geometry per colour slot**, cached per part like every other part and freed with the lab. The 26 existing parts keep their hand-written builders untouched; new plain bricks, plates, tiles, slopes and the big wheel reuse them. | One way to draw 191 new parts; a part is a few lines, `check.mjs` can read every one in node, and a piece stays one or a few draw calls (the low-end budget of brick-lab slice 13). Same idea as rails-as-data (brick-lab D17). |
| C2 | **Colour slots: `main` takes the palette, the rest are fixed.** A part's `main` slot is the colour the kid picked (a minifigure's shirt, a chair, a flag); other slots name a fixed finish (`skin`, `wood`, `glass`, `gold`, `fire`…). A part with no `main` slot is `fixed: true` and says "Comes in its own colours" on its info card — animals and plants come in their natural colours like Lego's moulded animals. Recolour still swaps only the palette material. | Kids choose the colour that matters (shirt, roof, car) without painting a horse purple by accident; minifig faces stay yellow. |
| C3 | **Palette 13 → 21 colours** (ids stable): lime, dark blue, azure, dark red, gold and silver (metallic), transparent clear and transparent blue (see-through, like Lego's trans bricks — windows, water, sci-fi). The colour grid at the foot of the rail keeps its height (3 rows) and scrolls sideways. | Castles want dark red and silver, pirates gold, space trans-blue; one more row would steal parts from short tablets. |
| C4 | **18 categories (8 → 18)**, in build order: Bricks, Plates, Slopes, Round 圓形, Structure, Doors & Windows 門窗, Wheels, Vehicle Parts 車輛零件, Connectors, Rails, Nature, Minifigures 人偶, Animals 動物, Accessories 配件, Home & Town 居家城鎮, Castle 城堡, Pirates 海盜, Space 太空. Category icons stay inside Emoji 5.0 so Android 8's font draws them. The list already scrolls inside the rail (brick-lab slice 12 fix); it now scrolls on every tablet. | Shape categories to build with, theme categories to find the fun bits; search (EN + 中文) still crosses all of them. |
| C5 | **Stacking reads part data, not shape names.** `support: false` — nothing rests on it (wheels, the old tree and flower); `ground: true` — plants straight into the baseplate (the old tree and flower; new plants stack like bricks so a bush can sit on a plate); `top` — the height other parts rest at when it is lower than the part (a saddle, a seat, a minifig's head under its hair); `sink` — how far a hat drops over a head when it lands on a part marked `head`. Old parts behave exactly as before. | A minifigure can ride a horse, sit on a chair or throne, wear a crown or a pirate hat — with the same tap-to-place rules. |
| C6 | **Minifigure scale is real:** 2 studs wide, 1 deep, about 3½ bricks tall; hats and helmets share the 2×1 footprint so they snap exactly onto a head. | They fit doors (1×4×6), seats and horses built to the same 8 mm grid. |
| C7 | **Tray previews are rendered from the real part** (lazily, a few per frame, into a small off-screen target with the lab's own GL context, in the active colour, cached per part + colour). The CSS sketch shows until the picture is ready and stays if it can't be made. Thumbnail frames are not counted by the slow-tablet step-down. | 191 more hand-drawn CSS previews is not possible; a picture of the actual part is what a pre-reader needs. |
| C8 | **No mechanics yet.** Levers, hinges, turntables, gears and propellers are shapes; nothing moves or turns on its own. | Keeps the build rules simple; moving parts are their own plan (see *Not in this plan*). |
| C9 | **Saved builds are untouched:** no existing id, colour id or footprint changed; a part id an older build doesn't know still falls back as before. | Brick-lab D6. |

## Not in this plan
Moving parts (levers that flip, wheels that roll, propellers that spin), parts held in a minifigure's hand, real hinge angles, trains, sticker/printed tiles, a builder for custom parts, sharing builds between tablets.
