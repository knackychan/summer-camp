## How To Read This Book

This book follows Minecraft as a living invention. It begins with one programmer testing block worlds in May 2009, then follows the game through Classic, Indev, Infdev, Alpha, Beta, the 2011 full release, Microsoft ownership, cross-platform play, classroom use, famous updates, community culture, and the modern game-drop era. The transcript supplied the spine of the story; the timeline and recent details were checked against official Minecraft and Microsoft pages, plus Guinness World Records for the current sales record.

本書把 Minecraft 視為一個仍在生長的發明來閱讀。故事從 2009 年 5 月一位程式設計師測試方塊世界開始，接著經過 Classic、Indev、Infdev、Alpha、Beta、2011 年正式版、Microsoft 收購、跨平台遊玩、教育用途、重要更新、玩家文化，以及近年的「遊戲掉落」更新模式。使用者提供的逐字稿提供了故事主軸；時間線和較新的資料則以 Minecraft、Microsoft 官方頁面，以及 Guinness World Records 的銷售紀錄交叉確認。

## Chapter 1: Before The Blocks

Minecraft did not appear from nowhere. Before the first public build, Markus Persson had already been experimenting with games and prototypes. He was a Swedish programmer who loved systems that could surprise their creators. The important idea was not simply "blocks." It was the combination of blocks, a first-person view, a world that could be changed, and rules loose enough for players to invent their own goals.

The transcript correctly points to `Infiniminer` as a key inspiration. Infiniminer, made by Zachtronics, showed how exciting it could feel to dig through a blocky world with other players. Other influences often mentioned around Minecraft's early design include `Dwarf Fortress`, with its deep simulated worlds, and `Dungeon Keeper`, with its underground spaces and indirect storytelling. Minecraft took pieces of that design weather and made something simpler, more physical, and easier to understand at a glance: break a block, keep it, place it somewhere else.

Minecraft 出現之前，並不是完全空白。第一個公開版本之前，Markus Persson 已經在嘗試各種遊戲和原型。他是一位瑞典程式設計師，喜歡能讓創作者自己也感到驚喜的系統。真正重要的想法不只是「方塊」，而是方塊、第一人稱視角、可改造世界，以及足夠開放到讓玩家自己設定目標的規則。

逐字稿正確指出 `Infiniminer` 是重要靈感來源。Zachtronics 製作的 Infiniminer 展示了多人一起挖掘方塊世界可以多有吸引力。Minecraft 早期設計常被提到的其他影響還包括 `Dwarf Fortress` 的深層模擬世界，以及 `Dungeon Keeper` 的地下空間和間接敘事。Minecraft 把這些設計氣味拆開，變成更簡單、更具體、更容易一眼理解的東西：打掉一個方塊，拿起來，再放到別的地方。

## Chapter 2: Cave Game, May 2009

The earliest Minecraft was called `Cave Game`. On May 17, 2009, the first public build appeared. It was not yet a finished commercial product, not yet a survival adventure, and not yet the giant cultural platform we know today. It was closer to a playable question: what if a world was made of blocks, and the player could move those blocks around?

That first question was powerful because it was instantly understandable. Many games tell the player exactly what to do. Minecraft began by asking, "What do you want to make?" At first, the world was plain, the number of blocks was tiny, and there was little formal structure. Yet the most important loop was already there: see material, change material, imagine a better shape, build it.

The early public release also mattered because Minecraft grew in conversation with players. Forum posts, screenshots, feedback, bugs, and suggestions became part of the development rhythm. The game was not hidden for years and revealed as a polished object. It was shown early, rough, and alive.

最早的 Minecraft 叫做 `Cave Game`。2009 年 5 月 17 日，第一個公開版本出現。它還不是完整商業產品，還不是生存冒險，也還不是今天龐大的文化平台。它更像是一個可以玩的問題：如果世界由方塊組成，而且玩家可以移動這些方塊，會發生什麼？

這個最初的問題很有力量，因為它一眼就能懂。許多遊戲會清楚告訴玩家要做什麼；Minecraft 一開始卻像是在問：「你想做什麼？」最初的世界很簡單，方塊種類很少，正式規則也不多。但最核心的循環已經存在：看見材料，改變材料，想像更好的形狀，然後建造。

早期公開版本也很重要，因為 Minecraft 是和玩家一起對話長大的。論壇文章、截圖、回饋、錯誤回報和建議，都成為開發節奏的一部分。這款遊戲不是被藏起來多年，最後以完美產品亮相；它很早就被展示出來，粗糙，但活著。

## Chapter 3: Classic, Survival Test, And The First Friction

Classic Minecraft was mainly about building. Players could place and remove blocks and use the world as a blank page. But a world made only for construction risks becoming too calm. Survival Test changed that. Health, enemies, danger, and consequences gave the blocks a new meaning. A wall was no longer just decoration; it was shelter. A hole in the ground was no longer just a shape; it could become a mine, a trap, or a desperate first-night hiding place.

This is where Minecraft discovered one of its strongest tensions: creative freedom becomes more dramatic when the world pushes back. The player can build anything, but night falls. The player can explore anywhere, but monsters wait. The player can dig deeper, but getting lost underground becomes part of the story.

The creeper belongs to this period of early invention. Its famous origin, a malformed pig model, is part of Minecraft legend because it shows how accidental ideas can become iconic when a designer notices their strange potential. The creeper is not just another monster. It is a design shortcut with perfect emotional timing: quiet approach, sudden hiss, explosion, regret, repair, and memory.

Classic Minecraft 主要是關於建造。玩家可以放置和移除方塊，把世界當成空白紙。但一個只為建造而存在的世界，也可能太平靜。Survival Test 改變了這件事。生命值、敵人、危險和後果讓方塊有了新的意義。牆不再只是裝飾，而是庇護所；地洞不再只是形狀，而可以是礦坑、陷阱，或第一晚慌忙躲藏的地方。

Minecraft 在這裡發現了最強的張力之一：當世界會反抗時，創造自由會更有戲劇性。玩家可以建造任何東西，但是夜晚會降臨。玩家可以去任何地方探索，但是怪物會等待。玩家可以越挖越深，但是在地下迷路也會成為故事的一部分。

苦力怕就屬於這段早期發明的時期。牠著名的來源，是一個變形的豬模型，之所以成為 Minecraft 傳說，是因為它顯示了偶然想法如何在設計者看見潛力後變成經典。苦力怕不只是另一種怪物。它是一個情緒時機完美的設計：安靜靠近、突然嘶聲、爆炸、後悔、修補，然後留下記憶。

## Chapter 4: Indev And Infdev

In late 2009, Minecraft entered `Indev`, short for "in development." The game gained more recognizable survival tools: crafting, smelting, tools, food, light, and a stronger day-night rhythm. These systems were not separate decorations. They interlocked. A player needed wood to make tools, tools to gather stone, stone to mine better material, fuel to smelt ore, and light to make dangerous spaces safer.

Then came `Infdev`, short for "infinite development." The promise was huge: the world could keep generating as the player traveled. Minecraft worlds are not truly infinite in the mathematical sense, but the practical effect was astonishing. The horizon became an invitation. A player could walk away from home and trust that the world would continue to unfold.

This shift gave Minecraft one of its great emotional structures: home and wilderness. Players build a base, leave it, risk losing it, return with resources, improve it, then leave again. Exploration and settlement became a loop. The game did not need a quest marker to create adventure. Distance itself became a story engine.

2009 年底，Minecraft 進入 `Indev`，也就是「in development」。遊戲取得了更像生存遊戲的工具：合成、熔煉、工具、食物、光源，以及更明確的日夜節奏。這些系統不是彼此分離的裝飾，而是互相咬合。玩家需要木頭做工具，用工具採石頭，用石頭挖更好的材料，用燃料熔煉礦物，用光讓危險空間變安全。

接著是 `Infdev`，也就是「infinite development」。它的承諾非常巨大：玩家走到哪裡，世界就能繼續生成到哪裡。Minecraft 世界在數學上不是真正無限，但實際感受非常驚人。地平線變成邀請。玩家可以離開家，並相信世界會繼續展開。

這個轉變給了 Minecraft 一個重要的情感結構：家與荒野。玩家建立基地，離開基地，冒著迷路風險，帶著資源回來，改善基地，然後再次出發。探索與定居形成循環。遊戲不需要任務標記也能產生冒險，距離本身就是故事引擎。

## Chapter 5: Alpha, Mojang, And The Nether

By 2010, Minecraft had become too large to remain a side project. Persson focused on it full time, Mojang formed, and the game entered its Alpha era. Alpha sharpened the feeling that Minecraft was both a survival game and a construction kit. Animals, hostile mobs, multiplayer survival, better terrain, minecarts, redstone basics, and many familiar blocks and items arrived in this period.

The Nether, introduced in the Halloween Update, was one of the first times Minecraft opened a completely different dimension. It changed the game's sense of scale. The Overworld was not all there was. A player could build a portal and step into a hostile underworld of lava, strange terrain, and new materials. This mattered not only because the Nether was dangerous, but because it proved Minecraft could contain worlds inside worlds.

Multiplayer also changed the meaning of Minecraft. In single-player, the world records one person's choices. In multiplayer, the world becomes a shared argument, a shared canvas, and sometimes a shared disaster. Survival servers, creative servers, mini-games, roleplay maps, and later massive networks all grew from this social seed.

到了 2010 年，Minecraft 已經太大，無法繼續只是副業。Persson 全職投入，Mojang 成立，遊戲進入 Alpha 時代。Alpha 讓 Minecraft 同時是生存遊戲與建築工具箱的感覺更加清楚。動物、敵對怪物、生存多人模式、更好的地形、礦車、紅石基礎，以及許多熟悉的方塊和物品，都在這段時間出現。

萬聖節更新加入的 Nether，是 Minecraft 第一次打開完全不同維度的重要時刻之一。它改變了遊戲的尺度感。主世界不是全部。玩家可以建造傳送門，走進充滿岩漿、奇異地形和新材料的敵意地下世界。這不只因為 Nether 危險，更因為它證明 Minecraft 可以在世界裡容納另一個世界。

多人模式也改變了 Minecraft 的意義。單人遊戲中，世界記錄一個人的選擇。多人遊戲中，世界變成共同爭論、共同畫布，有時也是共同災難。生存伺服器、創造伺服器、小遊戲、角色扮演地圖，以及後來的大型伺服器網路，都從這顆社交種子長出來。

## Chapter 6: Beta And The Road To 1.0

Beta began in December 2010. It was still unfinished, but by then Minecraft already had a serious community. The game was being sold, discussed, modded, streamed, documented, and copied. Players were no longer waiting for a normal release; they were living inside the development process.

The Beta period added many features that now feel like they were always there: beds, weather, wolves, maps, pistons, hunger, villages, ravines, strongholds, abandoned mineshafts, Endermen, enchantments, potions, and more. The game was moving from "survive and build" toward "survive, build, explore, automate, trade, fight, and tell stories."

The Adventure Update, especially Beta 1.8 and the later pre-releases, was a turning point. Minecraft gained more structure without becoming a linear game. Strongholds suggested mystery. Endermen gave the world a stranger kind of fear. Hunger changed survival from a simple health bar into a daily rhythm. Villages hinted that the world was inhabited, even if those early villagers were simple.

Beta 於 2010 年 12 月開始。它仍然未完成，但到那時 Minecraft 已經有了龐大社群。遊戲被販售、討論、改模組、直播、記錄，也被模仿。玩家不再只是等待一般意義上的正式發售；他們已經住在開發過程裡。

Beta 時期加入了許多現在看起來像一開始就存在的功能：床、天氣、狼、地圖、活塞、飢餓、村莊、峽谷、要塞、廢棄礦坑、終界使者、附魔、藥水等等。遊戲從「生存與建造」走向「生存、建造、探索、自動化、交易、戰鬥和說故事」。

Adventure Update，尤其是 Beta 1.8 和之後的預覽版本，是重要轉折。Minecraft 得到更多結構，但沒有變成線性遊戲。要塞暗示神祕感。終界使者讓世界有了一種更奇怪的恐懼。飢餓讓生存不再只是生命值，而變成日常節奏。村莊暗示世界有人居住，即使早期村民還很簡單。

## Chapter 7: The Official Release, November 18, 2011

Minecraft 1.0 launched on November 18, 2011. The release gave the game an official shape: The End, the Ender Dragon, enchanting, brewing, breeding, hardcore mode, and a credits sequence. This was unusual for a sandbox. Minecraft did not suddenly become a story game, but it now had something like a distant finish line.

That finish line changed the emotional arc. A player could still ignore it forever, but the Ender Dragon gave survival worlds a possible myth: prepare, gather rare materials, find a stronghold, enter The End, defeat the dragon, and return. Minecraft became a game where a child could build a house, an engineer could build a calculator, a speedrunner could chase world records, and a group of friends could spend months on one shared world.

The release also marked a deeper handoff. Minecraft was no longer only Notch's experiment. Jens "Jeb" Bergensten and the Mojang team became increasingly central to its future. The game had outgrown the lone-developer myth, even while that origin story remained part of its identity.

Minecraft 1.0 於 2011 年 11 月 18 日推出。這次發售讓遊戲得到正式形狀：終界、終界龍、附魔、釀造、繁殖、極限模式與片尾名單。這對沙盒遊戲來說很特別。Minecraft 並沒有突然變成劇情遊戲，但它現在有了一條遙遠的終點線。

這條終點線改變了情感曲線。玩家仍然可以永遠不理會它，但終界龍給生存世界一個可能的神話：準備、收集稀有材料、尋找要塞、進入終界、擊敗龍，然後回來。Minecraft 成為一種可以同時容納不同玩法的遊戲：孩子可以蓋房子，工程師可以造計算機，速通玩家可以追求世界紀錄，一群朋友可以在同一個世界待上好幾個月。

正式版也標誌更深的交接。Minecraft 不再只是 Notch 的實驗。Jens「Jeb」Bergensten 和 Mojang 團隊越來越成為未來的核心。遊戲已經長大，超越了一人開發者的神話，即使這個起源故事仍是它身份的一部分。

## Chapter 8: 2012-2013, Minecraft Learns New Languages

After 1.0, Minecraft could have simply added more blocks forever. Instead, the early official updates widened the grammar of the game.

Version 1.1 in early 2012 added new language support and spawn eggs. Version 1.2 raised the world height to 256 blocks and introduced jungles, ocelots, and iron golems. Version 1.3 brought villager trading, emeralds, and desert and jungle temples. Suddenly villages were not only scenery; they were economies. Exploration could lead to structures, not just landscapes.

The Pretty Scary Update added the Wither, witches, bats, zombie villagers, beacons, anvils, and command blocks. The Redstone Update in 2013 made automation more expressive with hoppers, comparators, daylight sensors, weighted pressure plates, and blocks of redstone. The Horse Update added horses, leads, carpets, hardened clay, coal blocks, and more. Then The Update that Changed the World reworked biome variety, adding landscapes such as mesas, roofed forests, savannas, and many new flowers and fish.

This period matters because it made Minecraft speak more dialects. Builders gained color and height. Redstone engineers gained logic. Explorers gained reasons to travel. Survival players gained bosses, tools, and danger. The same game became richer without choosing only one audience.

1.0 之後，Minecraft 原本可以只是永遠加入更多方塊。但早期正式更新其實擴大了遊戲的文法。

2012 年初的 1.1 加入更多語言支援和生怪蛋。1.2 把世界高度提高到 256 格，加入叢林、豹貓和鐵魔像。1.3 帶來村民交易、綠寶石、沙漠神殿和叢林神殿。村莊突然不只是風景，也有了經濟。探索不只會遇到地形，也會遇到結構。

Pretty Scary Update 加入凋零怪、女巫、蝙蝠、殭屍村民、烽火台、鐵砧和指令方塊。2013 年的 Redstone Update 用漏斗、比較器、日照感測器、測重壓力板與紅石方塊，讓自動化更有表達力。Horse Update 加入馬、拴繩、地毯、硬化黏土、煤炭方塊等。接著 The Update that Changed the World 重做生態域多樣性，加入惡地、黑森林、莽原、許多花和魚。

這段時期重要，是因為它讓 Minecraft 學會更多方言。建築玩家得到顏色和高度。紅石工程師得到邏輯。探索玩家得到旅行理由。生存玩家得到頭目、工具和危險。同一款遊戲變得更豐富，卻沒有只選一種玩家。

## Chapter 9: 2014, The Bountiful Update And Microsoft

In September 2014, Microsoft announced an agreement to acquire Mojang and the Minecraft franchise for $2.5 billion. Microsoft said Minecraft was already one of the most popular games in history and planned to keep it available across PC, iOS, Android, Xbox, and PlayStation. The deal changed the scale of the franchise. Minecraft was no longer just a runaway indie success; it became a pillar of a major technology company's gaming strategy.

The same year, Java Edition 1.8, The Bountiful Update, gave mapmakers and technical players a huge toolbox. It added and revised commands, introduced armor stands, banners, ocean monuments, guardians, rabbits, endermites, slime blocks, granite, diorite, and andesite. Some players remember the visible content first, but the invisible systems mattered too. Commands and mapmaking tools helped Minecraft become a platform for custom adventures.

The Microsoft era is sometimes described too simply, as if Minecraft stopped being Minecraft. The better reading is that its center of gravity changed. Mojang now had access to larger infrastructure, cross-platform ambitions, education projects, branding partnerships, and a longer corporate future. At the same time, the challenge became harder: how do you grow a game without sanding away the weirdness that made people love it?

2014 年 9 月，Microsoft 宣布以 25 億美元收購 Mojang 和 Minecraft 品牌。Microsoft 表示 Minecraft 已經是史上最受歡迎的遊戲之一，並計畫讓它繼續在 PC、iOS、Android、Xbox 和 PlayStation 上提供。這筆交易改變了品牌尺度。Minecraft 不再只是爆紅的獨立遊戲，而成為大型科技公司遊戲策略的重要支柱。

同一年，Java Edition 1.8，也就是 The Bountiful Update，給地圖作者和技術玩家龐大的工具箱。它新增並修改指令，加入盔甲座、旗幟、海底遺跡、守衛者、兔子、終界蟎、史萊姆方塊、花崗岩、閃長岩和安山岩。有些玩家會先記得看得見的內容，但看不見的系統同樣重要。指令與地圖工具幫助 Minecraft 成為自訂冒險的平台。

Microsoft 時代有時被說得太簡單，好像 Minecraft 從此不再是 Minecraft。更好的理解是：它的重心改變了。Mojang 取得更大的基礎建設、跨平台野心、教育專案、品牌合作與更長期的公司未來。同時，挑戰也更困難：如何讓遊戲長大，卻不磨掉讓玩家喜愛的奇特個性？

## Chapter 10: Combat, Education, Exploration, And Color

The 2016 Combat Update changed one of Minecraft's oldest habits: fast clicking. Cooldowns, shields, tipped arrows, lingering potions, the off-hand slot, and the elytra made combat slower and more deliberate. Some players loved the added depth. Others missed the old rhythm. That disagreement itself shows how wide Minecraft's audience had become. A combat change was not only a mechanical patch; it affected servers, PvP culture, mini-games, maps, and muscle memory.

Minecraft: Education Edition also launched in 2016, after Microsoft had acquired MinecraftEdu and built a classroom-focused version. It gave teachers tools for managing worlds and using Minecraft as a learning space. The idea was not that Minecraft magically teaches everything by itself. The value was that students could build, collaborate, test, revise, explain, and see abstract ideas become spatial.

The Frostburn Update added polar bears, strays, husks, magma blocks, bone blocks, and Nether wart blocks. The Exploration Update added woodland mansions, explorer maps, shulker boxes, llamas, and illagers. The 2017 World of Color update refreshed colored blocks, beds, concrete, glazed terracotta, and parrots. These updates made Minecraft more expressive, not only more dangerous. They gave players new reasons to decorate, organize, travel, and personalize.

2016 年的 Combat Update 改變了 Minecraft 最老的習慣之一：快速連點。攻擊冷卻、盾牌、藥箭、滯留藥水、副手欄與鞘翅，讓戰鬥變得較慢也較有判斷。有些玩家喜歡更深的戰鬥；有些玩家想念舊節奏。這種分歧本身說明 Minecraft 受眾有多大。戰鬥改動不只是機械修補；它影響伺服器、PvP 文化、小遊戲、地圖和肌肉記憶。

Minecraft: Education Edition 也在 2016 年推出。Microsoft 收購 MinecraftEdu 後，建立了面向教室的版本。它給老師管理世界的工具，也讓 Minecraft 成為學習空間。這個概念不是 Minecraft 會自動教會一切，而是學生可以建造、合作、測試、修改、解釋，並把抽象概念變成空間。

Frostburn Update 加入北極熊、流髑、屍殼、岩漿方塊、骨塊和地獄疙瘩塊。Exploration Update 加入林地府邸、探險家地圖、界伏盒、駱馬與災厄村民。2017 年的 World of Color 更新刷新彩色方塊、床、混凝土、釉陶和鸚鵡。這些更新讓 Minecraft 更有表達力，不只是更危險。它們給玩家新的裝飾、整理、旅行和個人化理由。

## Chapter 11: Bedrock, Better Together, And The Platform Problem

Minecraft's success created a strange problem: there was not just one Minecraft. Java Edition had grown from the original PC version. Pocket Edition, console editions, Windows 10 Edition, and other platform versions had their own histories and technical limits. For a family or group of friends, this could be frustrating. Owning Minecraft did not always mean being able to play together.

The Bedrock codebase and the Better Together approach tried to solve that by unifying many platforms and making cross-play normal across consoles, mobile, and Windows. Java Edition remained separate, partly because of its history, modding ecosystem, technical architecture, and PC community. Today, the split still matters. Java is deeply loved for mods, snapshots, servers, and old PC culture. Bedrock is vital for cross-platform families, marketplace content, controllers, mobile devices, and console play.

This is one of Minecraft's most important evolutions: it became both a game and a compatibility challenge. Mojang was no longer only designing mobs and blocks. It was maintaining identities across devices, input methods, storefronts, parental systems, realms, servers, creators, and communities.

Minecraft 的成功造成了一個奇怪問題：世界上不只一個 Minecraft。Java Edition 從原始 PC 版本長出來。Pocket Edition、主機版、Windows 10 Edition 和其他平台版本都有自己的歷史和技術限制。對家庭或朋友來說，這可能很麻煩。擁有 Minecraft 不一定代表可以一起玩。

Bedrock 程式碼基礎與 Better Together 方向，試圖統一許多平台，讓主機、手機和平板、Windows 之間的跨平台遊玩成為日常。Java Edition 仍然分開存在，部分原因是歷史、模組生態、技術架構與 PC 社群。直到今天，這個分裂仍然重要。Java 深受模組、快照、伺服器與老 PC 文化玩家喜愛。Bedrock 對跨平台家庭、Marketplace 內容、手把、行動裝置與主機遊玩很重要。

這是 Minecraft 最重要的演化之一：它不只是遊戲，也成為相容性挑戰。Mojang 不再只設計怪物與方塊。它還要維持跨裝置、輸入方式、商店、家長系統、Realms、伺服器、創作者與社群之間的身份。

## Chapter 12: Oceans, Villages, Bees, And The Nether Reborn

The 2018 Update Aquatic changed one of Minecraft's emptiest spaces. Before it, oceans were often wide blue distance: useful for travel, but thin on life. Update Aquatic added coral reefs, shipwrecks, ocean ruins, turtles, dolphins, fish, drowned, kelp, seagrass, bubble columns, and tridents. The ocean became a destination.

Village & Pillage in 2019 rebuilt villages into more varied settlements with professions, job-site blocks, raids, pillagers, ravagers, pandas, cats, foxes, and wandering traders. Villagers became more legible and useful. Defending a village became an event, not just a side effect of nighttime.

Buzzy Bees arrived later in 2019 with bees, beehives, honey bottles, honeycomb, honey blocks, and pollination behavior. It was smaller than some updates, but important as a tone marker. Minecraft could update by adding an ecological system, not only bosses and biomes.

The 2020 Nether Update was one of the most dramatic modern revisions. Netherite created a new material goal beyond diamond. Piglins, hoglins, striders, bastions, ruined portals, basalt deltas, crimson forests, warped forests, and soul sand valleys made the Nether feel like a real dimension rather than one repeated hazard. It became a place to live, trade, travel, and fear.

2018 年的 Update Aquatic 改變了 Minecraft 最空曠的空間之一。在那之前，海洋常常只是大片藍色距離：適合旅行，但生命很少。Update Aquatic 加入珊瑚礁、沉船、海底遺跡、海龜、海豚、魚、沉屍、海帶、海草、氣泡柱和三叉戟。海洋變成目的地。

2019 年的 Village & Pillage 把村莊重建成更多樣的聚落，有職業、工作站方塊、突襲、掠奪者、劫毀獸、熊貓、貓、狐狸與流浪商人。村民變得更容易理解，也更有用。保衛村莊變成事件，不只是夜晚的副作用。

2019 年稍後的 Buzzy Bees 加入蜜蜂、蜂箱、蜂蜜瓶、蜂巢、蜂蜜方塊與授粉行為。它比某些更新小，但作為語氣標記很重要。Minecraft 可以透過加入生態系統來更新，而不只是加入頭目與生態域。

2020 年的 Nether Update 是現代最戲劇性的改版之一。獄髓創造了超越鑽石的新材料目標。豬布林、疣豬獸、熾足獸、堡壘遺跡、毀壞傳送門、玄武岩三角洲、緋紅森林、扭曲森林和靈魂沙谷，讓 Nether 感覺像真正的維度，而不只是重複的危險。它成為可以居住、交易、旅行，也值得害怕的地方。

## Chapter 13: Caves & Cliffs And The Shape Of The World

Caves & Cliffs was so large that Mojang split it into two parts in 2021. Part I added blocks, mobs, and materials: copper, amethyst, spyglasses, lightning rods, glow squids, axolotls, goats, candles, deepslate, moss, dripstone, and more. It gave builders and collectors new toys before the deeper world-generation work arrived.

Part II changed the body of Minecraft itself. The world height expanded upward and downward. Mountains became taller and more varied. Caves became larger, stranger, and more layered. Ore distribution changed. The old vertical rhythm of Minecraft, surface, mine, bedrock, was replaced with a taller and deeper geography.

The significance of Caves & Cliffs is easy to miss if one lists only features. Minecraft worlds are emotional. A mountain is not just coordinates; it is where a player decides to build a base. A cave is not just noise generation; it is where fear, luck, orientation, and discovery meet. By changing terrain generation, Mojang changed the memory shape of every new world.

Caves & Cliffs 太大，所以 Mojang 在 2021 年把它拆成兩部分。第一部分加入方塊、怪物與材料：銅、紫水晶、望遠鏡、避雷針、發光魷魚、美西螈、山羊、蠟燭、深板岩、苔蘚、滴水石等等。它先給建築玩家和收集玩家新玩具，再讓更深層的世界生成工作登場。

第二部分改變了 Minecraft 世界的身體。世界高度向上和向下擴張。山變得更高且更多樣。洞穴變得更大、更奇異、更有層次。礦物分布也改變。Minecraft 舊有的垂直節奏：地表、礦坑、基岩，變成更高也更深的地理。

如果只列功能，很容易錯過 Caves & Cliffs 的意義。Minecraft 世界是情感性的。山不只是座標，而是玩家決定建基地的地方。洞穴不只是生成雜訊，而是恐懼、運氣、方向感和發現相遇的地方。改變地形生成，就等於改變每個新世界的記憶形狀。

## Chapter 14: The Wild Update

The Wild Update in 2022 brought two contrasting moods. Mangrove swamps, mud, frogs, tadpoles, allays, and chests in boats made the Overworld feel softer and more alive. The deep dark, ancient cities, sculk blocks, and the Warden made it feel more dangerous than ever.

The Warden was a different kind of Minecraft threat. It was not designed as a normal monster to farm or defeat casually. It was a pressure system. Sound, darkness, panic, and restraint became mechanics. The lesson of the deep dark is almost the opposite of early Minecraft bravado: sometimes survival means not fighting.

This update also showed Mojang's modern design caution. Minecraft has a huge audience, including very young players, technical players, speedrunners, casual builders, and server communities. A new feature must be exciting, readable, and compatible with a world that has been accumulating systems since 2009. The Warden works because it adds intensity without turning the whole game into a combat game.

2022 年的 The Wild Update 帶來兩種對比鮮明的氣氛。紅樹林沼澤、泥巴、青蛙、蝌蚪、悅靈與箱船，讓主世界更柔和、更有生命。深淵、遠古城市、伏聆方塊和伏守者，則讓它比以往更危險。

伏守者是另一種 Minecraft 威脅。它不是設計來讓玩家輕鬆刷怪或隨便擊敗的一般怪物。它是一個壓力系統。聲音、黑暗、恐慌與克制都變成機制。深淵的教訓幾乎和早期 Minecraft 的勇猛相反：有時候，生存代表不要戰鬥。

這次更新也顯示 Mojang 現代設計上的謹慎。Minecraft 有龐大受眾，包括很年幼的玩家、技術玩家、速通玩家、休閒建築玩家和伺服器社群。新功能必須令人興奮、容易理解，並且能與 2009 年以來累積的系統相容。伏守者成功，是因為它增加強度，卻沒有把整個遊戲變成戰鬥遊戲。

## Chapter 15: Trails & Tales And Player Identity

Trails & Tales in 2023 focused on expression, memory, and discovery. Archaeology let players brush suspicious sand and gravel for pottery sherds and other finds. Decorated pots made the world feel as if it had a past. Armor trims changed how equipment looked, letting players show identity even while wearing survival gear. Cherry groves added one of the game's most instantly loved landscapes.

Camels changed travel for two players at once. Bamboo blocks and rafts expanded building styles. Hanging signs gave builders better storytelling tools. The sniffer, chosen through community voting, added a prehistoric creature that searched for ancient seeds.

The title matters. Trails are paths through the world. Tales are the stories players tell afterward. Minecraft had always generated stories by accident. Trails & Tales made that idea explicit: the world is not only a resource field, but a memory field.

2023 年的 Trails & Tales 聚焦於表達、記憶與發現。考古讓玩家用刷子清理可疑沙子和可疑礫石，找出陶器碎片和其他物品。裝飾陶罐讓世界感覺像有過去。盔甲紋飾改變裝備外觀，讓玩家即使穿著生存裝備，也能展現身份。櫻花樹林加入了遊戲中最立刻受到喜愛的地景之一。

駱駝改變了兩位玩家一起旅行的方式。竹方塊和竹筏擴展建築風格。懸掛告示牌給建築玩家更好的說故事工具。由社群投票選出的嗅探獸，加入一種會尋找遠古種子的史前生物。

標題很重要。Trails 是世界中的路徑。Tales 是玩家事後講出的故事。Minecraft 一直都會偶然生成故事。Trails & Tales 把這個想法明確說出來：世界不只是資源場，也是記憶場。

## Chapter 16: From Annual Updates To Game Drops

For years, players expected a large annual update. That rhythm made each summer feel like a major event, but it also meant long waits and heavy pressure on each named release. In the newer model, Mojang uses smaller, themed `game drops` released several times per year. The official Minecraft updates page explains that game drops began in 2023 with Bats and Pots and continued through later releases such as The Garden Awakens, Chase the Skies, Mounts of Mayhem, and more.

This is an important structural change. A game drop can focus on one theme: wolf armor, bundles, a pale garden, new ambience, copper, baby mobs, sulfur caves. Smaller releases let Mojang ship more often, gather feedback, and keep Minecraft present throughout the year.

It also changes how players remember history. Instead of one giant chapter per year, modern Minecraft has a seasonal rhythm. Some drops are cozy, some technical, some dangerous, some decorative. The game now evolves more like a calendar than a single yearly festival.

多年來，玩家習慣期待大型年度更新。這種節奏讓每個夏天都像大事件，但也代表等待很長，而且每個命名更新背負很大壓力。在新的模式中，Mojang 使用較小、主題明確、每年多次推出的 `game drops`。Minecraft 官方更新頁說明，遊戲掉落從 2023 年的 Bats and Pots 開始，並延續到 The Garden Awakens、Chase the Skies、Mounts of Mayhem 等後續版本。

這是重要的結構變化。遊戲掉落可以專注在單一主題：狼盔甲、收納袋、蒼白花園、新氛圍、銅、幼年怪物、硫磺洞穴。較小的版本讓 Mojang 能更常推出內容、收集回饋，並讓 Minecraft 一整年都保持存在感。

它也改變玩家記憶歷史的方式。現代 Minecraft 不再是一年一個巨大篇章，而有了季節節奏。有些掉落溫馨，有些技術性強，有些危險，有些重視裝飾。遊戲現在更像日曆一樣演化，而不是一年一次的節慶。

## Chapter 17: 2024, Trials And Gardens

The transcript ends with Tricky Trials in 2024, and that update is indeed one of the important late-modern releases. It added trial chambers, vaults, trial spawners, the Breeze, the Bogged, ominous trials, trial keys, the mace, and the crafter. Trial chambers gave combat-oriented players a replayable underground challenge. The crafter mattered to technical players because automated crafting had been requested for years.

But 2024 did not stop there. Armored Paws added armadillos, armadillo scutes, wolf armor, and wolf variants. Bundles of Bravery made bundles a normal inventory tool and brought hardcore mode to Bedrock. The Garden Awakens added the pale garden, pale oak, resin, eyeblossoms, and the Creaking, a mob tied to a hidden heart block.

Together these drops show the new Minecraft pattern. One release can support pets and families. Another can support challenge dungeons. Another can support inventory quality-of-life. Another can add atmosphere and fear. Minecraft no longer needs every update to serve every playstyle equally, because updates arrive more often.

逐字稿以 2024 年的 Tricky Trials 作為結尾，而那確實是晚近 Minecraft 的重要版本之一。它加入試煉密室、寶庫、試煉生怪磚、旋風使者、沼骸、凶兆試煉、試煉鑰匙、重錘與合成器。試煉密室給喜歡戰鬥的玩家可重複挑戰的地下內容。合成器對技術玩家很重要，因為自動合成已經被要求多年。

但 2024 年並沒有在那裡停止。Armored Paws 加入犰狳、犰狳鱗甲、狼盔甲與狼變種。Bundles of Bravery 讓收納袋成為正式物品，並把極限模式帶到 Bedrock。The Garden Awakens 加入蒼白花園、蒼白橡木、樹脂、眼花與嘎枝怪，一種與隱藏心臟方塊相連的怪物。

這些掉落合在一起，顯示新的 Minecraft 模式。一個版本可以支援寵物與家庭玩法。另一個可以支援挑戰地城。另一個可以改善背包生活品質。另一個可以加入氣氛與恐懼。Minecraft 不再需要每次更新都平均服務所有玩法，因為更新更常來。

## Chapter 18: 2025, Spring, Skies, Copper, And Mounts

In 2025, Minecraft kept using the game-drop rhythm. Spring to Life focused on ambient variety, biome feeling, and small world details such as mob variants and environmental touches. It represented a softer side of evolution: not every change needs to be a boss or a dimension. Sometimes a world becomes more believable because it has more small differences.

Chase the Skies launched on June 17, 2025 for both Bedrock and Java. Its signature feature was the happy ghast, a flying mount raised from a dried ghast into a ghastling and then into a rideable creature. It also added harnesses, a player locator bar, new music, craftable saddles, and broader sky-focused play. At the same time, Bedrock received Vibrant Visuals, described by Minecraft as its biggest official visual refresh to date, with Java support still in progress.

The Copper Age, released for Java as 1.21.9 on September 30, 2025, expanded copper into tools, armor, decorations, copper chests, shelves, and copper golems. The copper golem was especially symbolic. It connected player affection, automation, oxidation, and the long history of mob voting and community desire.

Mounts of Mayhem released on December 9, 2025. It added the spear, mounted combat, hostile mounted enemies such as camel husks, zombie horsemen, nautilus and zombie nautilus mounts, Netherite horse armor, and underwater riding systems. Minecraft had long had horses and elytra, but this drop made movement and combat interact more directly.

2025 年，Minecraft 繼續使用遊戲掉落節奏。Spring to Life 聚焦在環境變化、生態域感受與小型世界細節，例如怪物變種與環境點綴。它代表演化中較柔和的一面：不是每個改變都需要是頭目或維度。有時候，世界因為有更多小差異而更可信。

Chase the Skies 於 2025 年 6 月 17 日在 Bedrock 和 Java 推出。它的招牌功能是快樂幽魂，一種從乾燥幽魂養成幽魂幼體，再成長為可騎乘生物的飛行坐騎。它也加入韁具、玩家定位列、新音樂、可合成馬鞍，以及更以天空為中心的玩法。同時，Bedrock 得到 Vibrant Visuals，Minecraft 稱其為至今最大的官方視覺更新，而 Java 支援仍在開發中。

The Copper Age 於 2025 年 9 月 30 日以 Java 1.21.9 形式推出，把銅擴展成工具、盔甲、裝飾、銅箱、架子與銅魔像。銅魔像特別有象徵性。它連接玩家情感、自動化、氧化，以及怪物投票和社群願望的長久歷史。

Mounts of Mayhem 於 2025 年 12 月 9 日推出。它加入長矛、騎乘戰鬥、駱駝屍殼、殭屍騎士、鸚鵡螺與殭屍鸚鵡螺坐騎、獄髓馬鎧和水下騎乘系統。Minecraft 很早就有馬和鞘翅，但這次掉落讓移動與戰鬥更直接地互相作用。

## Chapter 19: 2026, Tiny Takeover And Chaos Cubed

Tiny Takeover launched on March 24, 2026. It redesigned and expanded baby mobs, added new sounds, made name tags craftable, and introduced the golden dandelion as a way to keep baby mobs young. Its tone was deliberately playful. After years of caves, combat, bosses, and dimensions, Tiny Takeover celebrated personality and cuteness as legitimate parts of Minecraft's design.

Chaos Cubed followed on June 16, 2026. As of 2026-07-28, Minecraft.net lists Chaos Cubed as the latest official release. Java Edition 26.2 describes it as adding sulfur cubes, sulfur caves, sulfur and cinnabar block sets, sulfur spikes, potent sulfur, a "Bounce" music disc, new music tracks, the Friends List, and experimental Vulkan rendering support.

The sulfur cube is a particularly modern Minecraft idea. It is a mob, but also a physics object, a toy, a puzzle piece, and a building system. Feed it different blocks and its movement changes: bouncing, sliding, sticking, burning, or behaving differently depending on what it absorbed. That is very Minecraft. A creature is not only a creature; it is also a mechanic players can bend into games, machines, sports, traps, transport, and chaos.

Chaos Cubed also added sulfur caves, cinnabar and sulfur blocks, geysers, and Bedrock party features. The drop's title is honest: by 2026, Minecraft is not only about mining and crafting. It is about controlled chaos, shared experiments, and the pleasure of discovering that a simple block rule can become a hundred player-made games.

Tiny Takeover 於 2026 年 3 月 24 日推出。它重新設計並擴展幼年怪物，加入新聲音，讓命名牌可以合成，並引入金蒲公英，讓幼年怪物保持幼小。它的語氣刻意很玩樂。在多年洞穴、戰鬥、頭目和維度之後，Tiny Takeover 把個性與可愛也視為 Minecraft 設計中正當的一部分。

Chaos Cubed 接著於 2026 年 6 月 16 日推出。截至 2026-07-28，Minecraft.net 將 Chaos Cubed 列為最新官方版本。Java Edition 26.2 說明它加入硫磺方塊怪、硫磺洞穴、硫磺與硃砂方塊組、硫磺尖刺、強效硫磺、「Bounce」音樂唱片、新音樂、好友列表，以及實驗性 Vulkan 渲染支援。

硫磺方塊怪是一個非常現代 Minecraft 的想法。它是怪物，但也是物理物件、玩具、謎題零件和建築系統。餵它不同方塊，它的移動方式會改變：彈跳、滑行、黏住、發熱，或根據吸收的東西展現不同特性。這非常 Minecraft。一個生物不只是生物；它也是玩家能彎折成遊戲、機器、運動、陷阱、交通與混亂的機制。

Chaos Cubed 也加入硫磺洞穴、硃砂與硫磺方塊、間歇泉，以及 Bedrock 的派對功能。這個掉落的標題很誠實：到了 2026 年，Minecraft 不只關於挖礦與合成。它關於可控制的混亂、共同實驗，以及發現一條簡單方塊規則能變成一百種玩家自製遊戲的樂趣。

## Chapter 20: Minecraft Outside The Game

Minecraft's evolution is not limited to update numbers. The game became a language for video, education, architecture, engineering, speedrunning, livestreaming, and social play.

On YouTube and streaming platforms, Minecraft became one of the central games of creator culture. Survival series turned private worlds into public stories. Hardcore worlds turned risk into suspense. Building channels turned architecture into performance. Redstone channels turned engineering into theater. Speedruns turned seed knowledge, movement, crafting, and luck into a competitive discipline.

In schools, Minecraft Education turned the sandbox into a structured classroom tool. Teachers use it for collaboration, coding, history, environmental science, digital citizenship, engineering, and storytelling. Its educational strength comes from making ideas spatial and shared. Students can stand inside the thing they are studying or designing.

Minecraft also expanded into other games and media. Minecraft Dungeons translated the world into a dungeon-crawler. Minecraft Legends reimagined it as action strategy. `A Minecraft Movie` became the first big-screen live-action adaptation, released in North America on April 4, 2025 and internationally beginning April 2, 2025. Mojang and Netflix also announced an animated series in development. The block world had become a franchise world.

Minecraft 的演化不只限於版本號。這款遊戲成為影片、教育、建築、工程、速通、直播與社交遊玩的語言。

在 YouTube 和直播平台上，Minecraft 成為創作者文化的核心遊戲之一。生存系列把私人世界變成公共故事。極限世界把風險變成懸念。建築頻道把建築變成表演。紅石頻道把工程變成劇場。速通把種子知識、移動、合成與運氣變成競技項目。

在學校裡，Minecraft Education 把沙盒變成有結構的教室工具。老師用它進行合作、程式設計、歷史、環境科學、數位公民、工程與故事創作。它的教育力量來自於把想法變成空間且能共享。學生可以站在自己正在研究或設計的東西裡面。

Minecraft 也擴展到其他遊戲和媒體。Minecraft Dungeons 把世界轉譯成地城闖關。Minecraft Legends 把它重新想像成動作策略。`A Minecraft Movie` 成為第一部大銀幕真人改編電影，於 2025 年 4 月 4 日在北美上映，國際市場自 2025 年 4 月 2 日起上映。Mojang 和 Netflix 也宣布正在開發動畫影集。方塊世界已經成為品牌世界。

## Chapter 21: Why Minecraft Survived

Minecraft survived because its center is simple and its edges are endless. The center is easy to teach: punch tree, craft tool, mine stone, build shelter, survive night. The edges can stretch toward anything: a castle, a farm, a redstone computer, a roleplay town, a modpack, a classroom, a server economy, a movie, a speedrun, a family realm.

It also survived because it respects player authorship. Minecraft does not only deliver content to be consumed. It gives content that becomes material. A new block is not merely a collectible; it is an architectural word. A new mob is not merely an enemy; it is an ecological actor, a pet, a farm component, a joke, a danger, or a story trigger. A new command is not merely a developer feature; it can become a custom map.

Finally, Minecraft survived because it can be remembered personally. Almost every player has a first shelter, a first diamond, a first creeper disaster, a first portal, a first lost world, or a first shared base. The official history is important, but Minecraft's real archive is distributed across millions of private memories.

Minecraft 能活下來，是因為它的中心很簡單，邊界卻幾乎無限。中心很容易教：打樹、合成工具、挖石頭、蓋庇護所、熬過夜晚。邊界則能延伸到任何地方：城堡、農場、紅石電腦、角色扮演城鎮、模組包、教室、伺服器經濟、電影、速通、家庭 Realm。

它也能活下來，是因為它尊重玩家作者身份。Minecraft 不只是把內容交給玩家消費。它給的內容會變成材料。新方塊不只是收藏品，而是建築詞彙。新怪物不只是敵人，而可能是生態角色、寵物、農場零件、笑話、危險或故事觸發器。新指令不只是開發者功能，而可能變成自訂地圖。

最後，Minecraft 能活下來，是因為它能被個人記住。幾乎每位玩家都有第一個庇護所、第一顆鑽石、第一次苦力怕災難、第一座傳送門、第一個失去的世界，或第一個共同基地。官方歷史很重要，但 Minecraft 真正的檔案庫分散在數百萬人的私人記憶裡。

## Closing Page

Minecraft began as a question in a small prototype: what if you could reshape the world one block at a time? Seventeen years later, the answer is still expanding. The game has become a survival challenge, a construction set, a programming toy, a classroom, a social network, a stage, a film world, a marketplace, a modding culture, and a memory machine.

Its evolution is not only the story of what Mojang added. It is the story of what players did with those additions. Every update gives Minecraft more words, but the sentences still belong to the people who play.

Minecraft 起初是一個小原型裡的問題：如果你可以一次一個方塊地重塑世界，會發生什麼？十七年後，答案仍在擴張。這款遊戲已成為生存挑戰、建築套件、程式玩具、教室、社交網路、舞台、電影世界、市場、模組文化與記憶機器。

它的演化不只是 Mojang 加入了什麼的故事。它也是玩家如何使用那些新增內容的故事。每次更新都給 Minecraft 更多詞彙，但句子仍然屬於遊玩的人。
