Play the Tiny Takeover drop
===========================

The cutest drop in Minecraft history is out now!

Baby mobs on the loose! I repeat. Baby mobs on the loose!!! I’m counting over three dozen fluffier, stubbier, and dangerously adorable new versions of baby mobs causing cuteness overload in both [Bedrock Edition](/article/minecraft-26-10-bedrock-changelog "Click to read the full changelog of features for Bedrock") and Java Edition, which can only mean one thing – the Tiny Takeover drop has officially stormed Minecraft!

Head out across the Overworld with your friends\* to listen out for heart-melting sounds, craft name tags for your pets, and keep your babies forever young with the golden dandelion. Unsure of where to start? Scroll down and we’ll help you find the right baby mob.

[![A video thumbnail pf giant baby chicken seen from below](/content/dam/minecraftnet/games/minecraft/article-pictures/tiny_takeover_thumbnail_trailer_C.jpg)](https://youtu.be/7A5YWn33eps "A video thumbnail pf giant baby chicken seen from below")

Find the right baby mob for you
-------------------------------

The cuteness is overwhelming, and with so many baby mobs to find and bring back home to your base, we’ve tried to narrow it down for you. Answer these hard-hitting questions and you’ll be sure to find the tiny little soulmate perfect for you and your playstyle.

![A flowchart on how to find your perfect baby mob](/content/dam/minecraftnet/games/minecraft/article-pictures/mc_tinytakeover_editorial_launch_flowchart_final.jpg)

Ready or not, [Tiny Takeover](/updates/tiny-takeover-drop "Learn more about Tiny Takeover") is out now! [Download Minecraft](/choose-your-game "Click to learn more about purchasing Minecraft") and go play the new game drop for free now.

\*Multiplayer functionality on console requires a platform specific online subscription (sold separately).
