Public prototype

Minecraft grew where players could see it.

Forum posts, screenshots, experiments, and rough builds became part of the design conversation.

論壇文章、截圖、實驗和粗糙版本，都成為設計對話的一部分。

First loop

Break a block. Keep it. Place it again.

That simple action made Minecraft readable in seconds and expandable for years.

打掉方塊、帶走方塊、再放回世界。這個簡單動作讓 Minecraft 幾秒內就能理解，卻能擴展多年。

It did not begin as a story. It began as a question.

它一開始不是故事，而是一個問題：你想做什麼？

Freedom got sharper when the world pushed back.

A wall became shelter. A cave became risk. A portal became a promise that the Overworld was not everything.

當世界會反抗時，自由變得更有張力。牆成為庇護所，洞穴成為風險，傳送門則證明主世界不是全部。

Nether imagery can stand in for the moment Minecraft became a world with other worlds inside it.
