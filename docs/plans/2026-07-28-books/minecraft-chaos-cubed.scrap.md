Meet sulfur cubes
-----------------

Sulfur cubes are a new physics-driven mob coming to Minecraft with a curious appetite for... blocks! Found in sulfur caves, this curious mob transforms depending on which block the player feeds it. Experiment and discover how to send sulfur cubes bouncing, sliding, and more!

Explore the sulfur cave biome
-----------------------------

Discover new Minecraft caves, with shallow pools of water and glow lichen. Sulfur caves are filled with yellow blocks of sulfur, red blocks of cinnabar, sulfur spikes, and a curious mob called the sulfur cube!

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

### What is Chaos Cubed in Minecraft?

Chaos Cubed is a Minecraft summer game drop that introduces sulfur caves, the sulfur cube mob, sulfur and cinnabar blocks, geysers, and new physics-based gameplay systems.

### What new gameplay features are in the Chaos Cubed drop?

New gameplay features include the sulfur cube mob, the sulfur cave biome, new blocks like cinnabar and sulfur, the “Bounce” music disc, and more!

### Is Chaos Cubed multiplayer?

Yes. Chaos Cubed supports multiplayer gameplay across compatible Minecraft platforms.

### What does the sulfur cube do?

The sulfur cube changes its movement and behavior depending on which blocks players feed it. Also, the sulfur cube can absorb blocks like TNT, but keep it away from flammable objects…

### What are sulfur caves?

Sulfur caves are a new underground cave biome where you can find sulfur pools, cinnabar blocks, sulfur spikes, and sulfur cubes.

### Do sulfur and cinnabar blocks have gameplay uses?

Yes. Players can build with them creatively, and potent sulfur can also create bubbling pools and geysers.

### Are Chaos Cubed drop updates available in Java and Bedrock Editions?

Yes, Chaos Cubed drop updates are available in both editions of Minecraft. You can check out some of the new features right now through the Minecraft Preview program or through Java Edition Snapshots!

### Do I need to purchase anything to access Chaos Cubed drop content?

Nope! The Chaos Cubed drop content is a free update to your existing version of Minecraft.

### What Bedrock Edition bonus content is included with this game drop?

The Chaos Cubed game drop also comes with Parties for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition. Players on Xbox, PlayStation, and Windows can create a party to play together, use text chat to stay in contact, and move between worlds, servers, and more as a group.

### How do I download or access Chaos Cubed drop updates?

Simply update Minecraft via your preferred platform!

### Can I play Chaos Cubed drop features in Realms?

Yes! Chaos Cubed drop features will be available in single-player, local multiplayer, servers, and in Realms.

### Are Chaos Cubed drop updates available on console, mobile, and PC?

Yes. Chaos Cubed drop updates are available on all current versions of Minecraft.
