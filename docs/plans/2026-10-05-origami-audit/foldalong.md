# Fold-along — the six ★ models (slice 07 checkpoint)

**For Papa.** Fold each model with a real square of origami paper, **white side up**, using only the
animation: no reading ahead, no looking anything up. Pause or Replay as much as you like. Then fill
in the row. Only when all six come out right are the other 22 models planned (design O1).

Open Origami Atelier on the tablet (or in the browser) and pick the model from the library.

## Questions only your paper can answer

1. **Paper Cup step 5 (the back rim).** It is now a mountain fold: "Fold the back top flap down
   behind the cup", so the view stays on the front. The other way is to turn the cup over and do a
   valley fold. Which one did your hands want to do?
2. **Swimming Fish step 4 (the tail).** The long point of the kite folds out to the side as a tail.
   It is the weakest of the six: the tail is narrow, and the fish swims upward. Does it read as a
   fish, or should the fish be redesigned?
3. **Cat Face step 3 (the forehead) comes before the ears now.** If the forehead folds after the
   ears, the ears barely stick out above the head. Does folding it first feel natural?
4. **Fox and Rabbit are folded the same way:** ears up on creases from the bottom point, then turn
   over. The fox's ears are short and lean out; the rabbit's are tall with a gap between them. Are
   they different enough?
5. **Where the creases go.** The animation has to pick one spot for a fold the words call "a
   little" (fox muzzle, cat chin, fish nose). Was it clear enough how far to fold?

## What changed in the words (EN + 中文)

- Every model starts as a diamond, white side up; step 1's hint says so.
- Little Fox: the two "narrow the face" steps are gone (they would fold the ears again); "Turn the
  fox over" is its own step, and then the muzzle. 7 → 6 steps.
- Dog Face: the nose (only the top layer of the bottom point folds up) and then the chin (the
  layer behind folds back). The old "top point backward" was a point the triangle doesn't have.
- Cat Face: long edge at the bottom, as the old hint said; forehead before the ears; the chin is a
  thin strip at the bottom folded back (the triangle has no bottom point).
- Swimming Fish: "fold into a triangle" and "open it again" are one fold-and-open step. 8 → 7 steps.
- Rabbit Face: step 5 is "Turn the rabbit over" (the old "top edge backward" would have folded the
  ears behind).
- Paper Cup: long edge at the **bottom** (the classic cup; the old hint said top); step 5 as in
  question 1.
